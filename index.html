About

I am a fourth-year Cognitive Science Ph.D. candidate at Stony Brook University working with Dr. Gregory Zelinsky.

My research interests are in the intersection of multimodal generative modelling and visual perception, focusing on building image and video generative models that are grounded in human visual behavior.

Since May 2026 I have been a Research Scientist Intern at Tavus, where I work on autoregressive video diffusion and distillation for real-time conversational avatars.

I am always interested in research collaborations. Feel free to schedule a 30-minute meeting if you would like to collaborate or talk through research ideas.
